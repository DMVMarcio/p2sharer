PRIVACY POLICY
P2Sharer — https://p2sharer.com
Last updated: November 21, 2026

P2Sharer is built so that what you share in a room goes directly to the other
participants. This policy explains what stays on your device, what other people and
services can see, and the choices you have. It covers the website, the web version and
the Windows app.

IN SHORT
- No account, email or phone number is required.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or telemetry.
- Messages, media and files travel directly between participants, encrypted end to end.
- Your profile, settings and saved rooms stay on your own device.

1. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
P2Sharer keeps the following locally, in your browser storage or in the app folder on
Windows. We have no access to it.
- Your display name, avatar, banner and nickname style.
- Saved rooms, invitations and the cryptographic keys that sign them.
- Settings, language, shortcuts, saved video queues and other preferences.
- On Windows, the shared browser profile (cookies, history and extensions) and the files
  you choose to save.

2. WHAT OTHER PARTICIPANTS RECEIVE
When you join a room, participants receive your profile and whatever you choose to
share: messages, files, screen, camera, audio and app activity. Files are only
transferred after the recipient accepts them. As with any direct WebRTC connection,
participants can see the network addresses used to reach you. Share invitations only
with people you trust; rooms can require a password and host approval.

3. P2SHARER SERVERS
Our server at p2sharer.com hosts the website and a signaling broker that helps devices
find each other. It only relays short connection messages under hashed room topics and
never stores messages, media or files. Your IP address is kept in memory only while you
are connected, to limit abuse, and is not written to logs. The downloads page reads
release information from GitHub through our server. P2Sharer sets no cookies of its own,
and our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Some features rely on external services. They receive your IP address and the data they
need to work, under their own privacy policies:
- Public discovery relays (MQTT brokers, Nostr relays and WebTorrent trackers) that the
  Windows app uses to find peers through hashed room topics. You can change them in
  settings.
- Google and Cloudflare STUN servers, used to discover a direct connection route, and
  any TURN server you configure.
- YouTube and Twitch players, YouTube title lookups, public Invidious instances for
  search and playlists, and Twitch sign-in if you choose to use it. Embedded players may
  set their own cookies.
- Websites opened in the shared browser, Google as its start page, and the Chrome Web
  Store or Edge Add-ons when you install extensions.
- GitHub, for downloads and for update checks in the Windows app.

5. WINDOWS APP PERMISSIONS
Your screen, audio and camera are captured only after you start sharing. Remote control,
virtual gamepads and the virtual network require your explicit consent, and
administrator features also ask for Windows confirmation. The app checks GitHub for
updates automatically; you can turn this off in settings, and updates are installed only
when you choose.

6. YOUR CHOICES
You can edit your profile, remove saved rooms, clear shared browser data, revoke remote
control at any time and change discovery services in settings. Clearing this site's data
in your browser or uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally.

7. CHILDREN
P2Sharer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive their
personal data.

8. CHANGES AND CONTACT
We will update this page whenever the way P2Sharer handles data changes. For questions,
open an issue on GitHub. Report security concerns privately through GitHub security
advisories.
