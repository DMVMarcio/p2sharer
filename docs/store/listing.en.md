# Microsoft Store listing — English (en-us)

## Product name
P2Sharer

## Short description
Private peer-to-peer rooms to share your screen in up to 4K 120 FPS, watch YouTube and Twitch in sync, send files of any size, play together and control PCs remotely. Free, open source and no account needed.

## Description
P2Sharer connects you directly to your friends. Create a room, share the invitation and everything you share — screens, audio, cameras, messages and files — travels straight between participants, encrypted end to end. No account, no subscription and nothing locked behind a paywall.

STREAM IN HIGH QUALITY
Share multiple screens, windows and cameras at up to 4K and 120 FPS with hardware encoding. Choose exactly which application's audio goes into the stream, and draw or point live on top of the shared screen.

PLAY TOGETHER
Hand over mouse, keyboard or a gamepad with one consent prompt. Every player gets their own virtual Xbox 360 controller, with stick deadzone, button remapping and per-controller profiles. Or start a match of Chess, Checkers or Tic-Tac-Toe right inside the room.

WATCH IN SYNC
Open YouTube and Twitch players for the whole room. Pausing or seeking updates everyone, and you can build shared queues and import playlists.

SEND FILES WITHOUT LIMITS
Send photos or multi-gigabyte archives directly between devices. Nothing is uploaded to a server: no storage quotas, no expiring links, and every transfer is verified.

CREATE AND EXPLORE
Draw on a multi-layer whiteboard, co-edit notes with Markdown and code blocks, browse websites together in a shared browser, and connect local game servers through TCP/UDP tunnels or a virtual LAN.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your profile, settings and saved rooms stay on your device. Rooms can require a password and host approval. P2Sharer has no ads, analytics or telemetry and is open source under the MIT license.

People without the app can join the same rooms from a web browser at p2sharer.com.
